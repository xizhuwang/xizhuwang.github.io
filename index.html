GRADUATE RESEARCH

Adaptive Mixed-Precision Architecture for Multi-User Large Language Model Inference on HBM-Based Processing-in-Memory

The research explores how concurrent LLM requests share HBM-PIM resources under bandwidth, memory-capacity and latency constraints. It combines adaptive precision, scheduling and data mapping to improve effective throughput while preserving inference quality.
