RECONFIGURABLE COMPUTE

Precision is another control dimension.

My research reconfigures compute resources and precision for AI workloads. The goal is to balance throughput, memory traffic and task quality.

01 / PRECISION × QUALITY

Precision changes the trade-off.

100.0%GSM8K quality retention

FP16 is the quality reference. INT4 trades some quality for higher peak throughput in this example.

02 / PRECISION ROOFLINE

Compute ceiling meets bandwidth.

INT3 · 221.5INT4 · 176.9FP8 · 88.5FP16 · 44.3 TOPS

In this eight-stack model, precision sets the compute ceiling. Arithmetic intensity and bandwidth determine the achievable limit.

01 / DVFS

Voltage & frequency

Changes execution speed and power while typically keeping the same numerical format.

02 / RECONFIGURATION

Precision & compute resources

Changes bit width and compute organization. Throughput and data traffic may improve, but numerical error must be evaluated.

These controls can be combined; precision adaptation does not replace DVFS.
